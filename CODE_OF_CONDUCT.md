# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and considerate.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and coordinated-disclosure boundaries.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Prioritize the safety and well-being of the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Publishing another person’s private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption, intimidation, or sustained bad-faith engagement.
- Public disclosure of sensitive vulnerability details before a coordinated release.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, and community events.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `security@threatflux.ai` with “Code of Conduct” in the subject. If the report also contains sensitive security information, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers will review reports in context, protect reporter privacy where practical, and may edit or remove content, reject contributions, issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Project maintainers are responsible for applying this policy fairly and consistently. Retaliation against someone who makes a good-faith report is not tolerated.
